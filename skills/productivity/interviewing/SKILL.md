---
name: interviewing
description: Interview the user about a plan or model, one question at a time with a recommended answer, until nothing is left open. Used by interview-model and calibrate.
---

Ask until you and the person understand the plan the same way.

1. One question at a time. Wait for the answer.
2. Give your recommended answer and why.
3. If the repo, issues or glossary already answer it, read them instead of asking.
4. If an answer clashes with the literature or an earlier answer, say so.
5. Ask more when a wrong guess is costly, decide and flag when it is cheap.
6. Keep a one-line log of each decision. Show it at the end, with the assumptions you made.

If nobody can answer, take the most reasonable reading, say so at the top, and go on. Never decide alone: the intended-use level (L1), going beyond a source's valid range, loosening a tolerance, the fluid and reference state. List these as open and stop there.
